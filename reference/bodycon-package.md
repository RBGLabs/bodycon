# bodycon: Estimation of Animal Body Condition Indices

Calculates animal body condition indices from measurements of body size
and mass. Methods include residual-based ordinary least squares (OLS),
the scaled mass index (SMI) using standardised major axis (SMA)
regression, and SMI using robust SMA regression. Visualisation tools
support comparison of indices and assessment of the suitability of these
approaches. See Krebs and Singleton (1993)
[doi:10.1071/ZO9930317](https://doi.org/10.1071/ZO9930317) , Peig and
Green (2009)
[doi:10.1111/j.1600-0706.2009.17643.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0706.2009.17643.x)
, and Warton et al. (2012)
[doi:10.1111/j.2041-210X.2011.00153.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2041-210X.2011.00153.x)
.
